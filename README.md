# Master-Piece-CSS

☀️ SHAMSTECH JO — Solar Energy Services & Solutions PlatformAn interactive, user-centric digital platform designed to streamline solar energy adoptability across Jordan by providing accurate ROI forecasting, system comparisons, and seamless service booking.📌 Project OverviewSHAMSTECH JO is a comprehensive Web UI/UX solution created for residential, commercial, and agricultural sectors. The platform guides users through their solar journey—from financial feasibility assessment and ROI projections to booking installation, maintenance, cleaning, and equipment services.🎯 Core Objectives:Empower Decision-Making: Provide an interactive Solar Savings & ROI Calculator tailored to regional energy tariffs (JOD).Demystify Solar Tech: Clear, modern breakdowns of solar configurations (On-Grid, Off-Grid, Hybrid).Streamline Operations: Fast, direct service request flows for certified engineering partners and contractors.🎨 Brand Identity & UI SystemPrimary Palette: Deep Slate Navy & Dark Forest Green (#0b1712, #13271d) establishing a sleek dark-mode aesthetic.Accent Colors: Amber/Gold (#f59e0b) representing solar energy & high-value CTAs; Emerald Green (#10b981) highlighting financial gains and sustainability.Bilingual Support: Native Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic interface with clean English technical terms and tags.🛠️ Key Features & ComponentsSolar Savings & ROI Calculator:Dynamic inputs for monthly electric bills (JOD) and sector tariffs.Real-time calculation of recommended capacity ($kWp$), annual financial savings, payback period (years), and 25-year cumulative profits.Interactive financial payback curve rendered with Chart.js.Installation Services Suite:Rooftop & Ground-Mount Systems: Turnkey design, permitting, net-metering paperwork, and commissioning.System Configurations:On-Grid Systems: Grid-tied for immediate bill reduction via net-metering.Off-Grid Systems: Battery-backed independent power for remote areas.Hybrid Systems: Grid connection combined with smart emergency backup storage.Maintenance & Care:Scheduled panel cleaning to optimize generation efficiency.On-demand diagnostic and repair workflows.💻 Tech StackFrontend: HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS.Logic & Analytics: JavaScript (ES6+), Chart.js for data visualization.UI/UX Design: Figma Vector Assets & SVG Iconography.🚀 Quick StartOpen index.html in any modern web browser.Enter a monthly bill value to test real-time ROI generation and view the 25-year payback projection graph.
